🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Recognize and produce the 7 days of the week
  • Recognize and produce the 12 months of the year
  • Recognize and use 8 basic weather adjectives
  • Match spoken words to images (listening comprehension)
  • Put days and months in the correct order

📚 Target Vocabulary

Weather: sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, snowy, stormy, hot, cold

Days: Monday–Sunday

Months: January–December

🕒 Suggested Use

  • Sessions of 15–20 minutes, 2–3 times per week
  • Works for individual practice, pair work, whole-class play on an interactive whiteboard, or homework
  • Start every lesson with the flashcards ("Learn" tab) before playing the games
  • Use the Word Search and Final Challenge as end-of-unit review

🧠 Skills Practiced

Listening, vocabulary recognition, sequencing, spelling recognition, and simple production through repetition.
